WebGPU tests

checking
  1. adapter.html: is there an adapter, and its info, features and limits
  2. device.html: making devices, default limits, each feature, destroy and lost
  3. compute.html: compute shaders and buffer readback, with timings
  4. offscreen-readback.html: rendering into textures and reading them back (MSAA, depth, float, mips)
  5. errors.html: error scopes, uncaptured errors, shader messages, pipeline errors
  6. triangle.html: a turning triangle on screen, frame rate after 30 frames
  7. cube.html: a textured, depth-tested cube, frame rate after 120 frames
  8. resize.html: canvas sizes from 16x16 to 1920x1080, unconfigure, rgba16float, a second device
  9. two-canvases.html: three canvases on two devices, one taken out of the page and put back
  10. todataurl.html: toDataURL, toBlob, createImageBitmap and drawImage of a WebGPU canvas, alpha modes
  11. copy-external-image.html: textures from canvases, bitmaps and images, flipY and premultiplied alpha
  12. video-texture.html: a playing video as an external texture (needs ../media/sync-1080p30-h264.mp4 or ?src=)
  13. buffer-map.html: buffer mapping as ML libraries use it, up to 64 MiB
  14. ml-transformers.html: a sentence-embedding model on WebAssembly and on WebGPU (needs the network, or ?models=http://host:8765/)
Every page runs on load and ends with a "summary" line; failures are red.
